Things to Do in Sarasota

Your guide to the best events, nightlife, and entertainment in Sarasota, FL

What is there to do in Sarasota this weekend?

Sarasota has incredible events every weekend including pool parties at The Sarasota Modern, live music downtown, the famous Siesta Key Drum Circle on Sundays, and the Downtown Farmers Market on Saturdays.

What is the best nightlife in Sarasota?

Downtown Sarasota and St. Armands Circle have the best nightlife. Top spots include rooftop bars at The Sarasota Modern, beach clubs on Siesta Key, and live music venues downtown.

Are there any pool parties in Sarasota?

Yes! The Sarasota Modern hosts rooftop pool parties with live DJs, craft cocktails, and stunning views. The biggest event is Stars, Stripes & Sunsets on July 4th.

What is happening in Sarasota for July 4th 2026?

The biggest July 4th event is Stars, Stripes & Sunsets at The Sarasota Modern - a rooftop pool party from 2-8pm with 4 live DJs. Tickets are $10 in advance.
