⚡ Stop Taking Free Requests

Turn every song request into revenue. Your crowd pays, you play, everyone wins.

🎵 How It Works

  1. Start a Session — Select your venue and set your base request fee
  2. Display Your QR Code — Put it at your booth, on screen, anywhere visible
  3. Fans Scan & Pay — They request songs and pay instantly via their phone
  4. You Get Paid — Money hits your Stripe account automatically
