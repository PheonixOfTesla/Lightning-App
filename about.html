Our Story

Lightning Pass started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find out what's happening in Sarasota tonight?

We saw a city full of incredible venues, talented DJs, and amazing events - but no central place to discover them all. So we built one.

Today, Lightning Pass is Sarasota's home for events, nightlife, and experiences. From the Siesta Key Drum Circle to downtown rooftop parties, we're your pass to everything.

"Skip the line. Own the night."
